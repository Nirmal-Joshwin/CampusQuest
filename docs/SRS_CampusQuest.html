1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document describes the complete requirements for Version 1.0 of CampusQuest: Location-Based Augmented Reality (AR) Campus Exploration & Gamified Engagement System. The objective of this document is to define the functional, non-functional, behavioral, and architectural requirements of the system and establish a rigorous baseline for development, validation, and academic evaluation at Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT).

This document is intended for project supervisors, system evaluators, software engineers, security auditors, campus administrators, and end-user students. Developers should consult Section 3 (External Interfaces) and Section 4 (System Features). Testers and evaluators should inspect Section 5 (Nonfunctional Requirements) and the UML analysis models in Appendix B.

1.4 Product Scope

CampusQuest is a mobile multiplayer location-based MMORPG and campus discovery platform engineered specifically for the Coimbatore Institute of Technology campus. The application transforms the physical 25-acre CIT campus into an interactive digital augmented reality realm. Students physically navigate the campus grounds to detect cybernetic anomalies, uncover historical and academic lore at canonical landmarks, collect quantum loot caches, participate in collaborative multiplayer tag-team raids, and compete on department leaderboards. By tying gameplay mechanics directly to physical movement within validated geographic boundaries, CampusQuest fosters active student wellness, peer collaboration, campus spatial orientation, and inter-departmental community engagement.

2. Overall Description

2.1 Product Perspective

CampusQuest operates as a distributed client-server ecosystem connecting mobile student clients with a high-performance backend server, spatial verification engines, and cloud vector map providers. The system replaces passive campus navigation with an active, gamified location-based exploration platform.

2.2 Product Functions

  • Provides secure role-based authentication with institutional department selection (CSE, ECE, MECH, CIVIL, IT, AI&DS).
  • Renders an interactive 3D vector map with 55° camera tilt and volumetric building extrusion rendering.
  • Computes real-time geodesic distances using the Haversine formula triggering encounters within 15 meters.
  • Validates all client interactions against an immutable 23-point CIT campus polygon using ray-casting geometry.
  • Provides an optical AR camera viewfinder with animated capture reticles and procedural audio.
  • Maintains a persistent Bestiary inventory of captured anomalies with companion buddy equipping.
  • Simulates an in-game Campus Data Credit economy with collectible quantum loot crates and an armory shop.
  • Supports real-time multiplayer proximity discovery, radar pinging, and cooperative tag-team raids.
  • Equips administrators with spatial telemetry, player density monitoring, and dynamic spawn manipulation.

2.3 User Classes and Characteristics

  • Student Cadet: Enrolled students who use the mobile application to explore campus, discover anomalies, and participate in squad challenges.
  • Administrator / Gamemaster: Faculty coordinators or game masters with privileged access to telemetry dashboards, spawn population, and cheating audits.
  • Peer Cadet / Squad Member: Concurrent players discovered in proximity for tag-team raids and mutual radar beaconing.

2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints

  • Geographic Boundary: Gameplay is strictly confined to the digitized 23-point perimeter of Coimbatore Institute of Technology (11.0254°N – 11.0305°N, 77.0259°E – 77.0291°E).
  • GPS Jitter Buffer: A 15-meter buffer margin is enforced along boundary edges to accommodate satellite multipath errors near multi-story academic buildings.
  • Safe Area Context: All screens strictly utilize react-native-safe-area-context for notch and gesture bar accommodation.

2.6 User Documentation

  • Cadet Field Guide (in-app onboarding tutorial).
  • Administrator Spatial Operations Manual.
  • Interactive OpenAPI/Swagger documentation hosted natively at /docs.

2.7 Assumptions and Dependencies

  • Cadet devices have active GNSS location services enabled with High Accuracy mode.
  • Camera permissions are granted for AR viewfinder rendering.
  • Campus Wi-Fi or cellular 4G/5G data connectivity is operational.

3. External Interface Requirements

3.1 User Interfaces

The interface implements a high-contrast Cyber-Glass visual theme with liquid translucent panels optimized for outdoor sunlight visibility:

  • Authentication Screen (login.tsx): Tabbed login/registration with institutional department picker.
  • 3D Tactical Map HUD (index.tsx): 55° pitched 3D hybrid map with extruded buildings, custom cadet marker, 15m radar circles, compass heading indicator, and centering controls.
  • AR Viewfinder & Catch Arena (catch.tsx): Live camera viewfinder rendering floating 3D anomalies, targeting reticles, capture slider, and procedural sound feedback.
  • Bestiary & Inventory Hub (inventory.tsx): Tabbed catalog of captured anomalies, lore descriptions, and "Set as Active Buddy" controls.
  • Cadet Squad & Radar (friends.tsx): Real-time proximity list, one-tap radar ping broadcasting, and friend management.
  • Campus Armory (shop.tsx): Item merchant offering capture modules, energy cells, and avatar titles.
  • Administrator Console (admin.tsx): Telemetry counters, spawn repopulation triggers, and campus reset utilities.

3.2 Hardware Interfaces

  • GNSS Receiver: Sampled periodically to acquire real-world latitude, longitude, and heading.
  • Optical Camera: Accessed at 30fps to render real-world background video during AR encounters.
  • Haptic Engine: Triggers tactile vibrations on taps, successful captures, and out-of-bounds alerts.
  • Audio Hardware: Outputs procedurally synthesized WebAudio sound effects.

3.3 Software Interfaces

  • FastAPI & Uvicorn: High-speed ASGI server delivering sub-millisecond serialization.
  • SQLAlchemy ORM: Object-relational mapping with foreign-key enforcement.
  • Expo Modules: expo-camera, expo-location, expo-audio, and expo-haptics.

3.4 Communications Interfaces

  • Protocol: HTTPS / TLS 1.3 for encrypted client-server exchanges.
  • Authentication: Bearer tokens transmitting HMAC-SHA256 signed JWTs.
  • Format: UTF-8 encoded JSON conforming to strict Pydantic models.

4. System Features

4.1 Cadet Registration and Authentication Management

Priority: High
Enables students to register with institutional credentials, select their academic department, and securely authenticate.

Requirement IDRequirement Description
REQ-001The system shall allow new cadets to register with email, username, department, and password.
REQ-002The system shall validate username formats against the pattern ^[a-zA-Z0-9_.-]+$.
REQ-003The system shall reject duplicate email addresses or usernames with HTTP 400 Bad Request.
REQ-004The system shall authenticate user credentials using BCrypt hashing and issue signed JWT bearer tokens.
REQ-005The system shall persist student session state and automatically redirect authenticated users to the Map HUD.

4.2 Geospatial 3D Map and Real-Time Radar HUD

Priority: High
Renders an interactive 3D perspective of Coimbatore Institute of Technology with extruded buildings and entity pins.

Requirement IDRequirement Description
REQ-006The system shall render an isometric 3D map with forward pitch of 55° and altitude of 380m.
REQ-007The system shall automatically extrude volumetric 3D building footprints using vector tile map layers.
REQ-008The system shall display the player's position using a custom Cadet marker and interaction radar circle.
REQ-009The system shall render canonical story spawns and dynamic loot crates within the CIT campus perimeter.
REQ-010The system shall compute real-time geodesic distances using the Haversine formula and update UI indicators.

4.3 Anomaly Encounter and Camera-Based AR Capture

Priority: High
Enables cadets within 15 meters of an anomaly to initiate an optical AR encounter and execute capture mini-games.

Requirement IDRequirement Description
REQ-011The system shall restrict AR encounter initiation to instances where cadet-anomaly distance is ≤ 15m.
REQ-012The system shall stream live camera frames at 30fps as the background canvas for AR encounters.
REQ-013The system shall render animated targeting reticles and interactive capture controls over the optical feed.
REQ-014The system shall compute capture success probabilities determined by creature rarity tier and cadet level.
REQ-015The system shall award experience points (100 to 500 XP) and Data Credits upon successful capture.

4.4 Geofenced Anti-Spoofing and Ray-Casting Boundary Validation

Priority: High
Enforces strict spatial security rules preventing unauthorized captures from outside CIT campus boundaries.

Requirement IDRequirement Description
REQ-016The system shall maintain an immutable 23-vertex polygon perimeter defining the CIT campus.
REQ-017The system shall evaluate capture coordinates using an algebraic Ray-Casting Point-in-Polygon (PIP) algorithm.
REQ-018The system shall incorporate a calibrated 15-meter buffer margin along boundary edges to mitigate GPS jitter.
REQ-019The system shall reject any capture or loot collection occurring outside campus boundaries with HTTP 400.
REQ-020The system shall validate coordinates at the Pydantic schema level to block out-of-region spoofing with HTTP 422.

4.5 Bestiary Inventory and Companion Buddy System

REQ-023The system shall categorize Bestiary entries into Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary rarity tiers.
REQ-024The system shall permit students to equip any captured creature as their active roaming buddy companion.
REQ-025The system shall render the active buddy companion alongside the cadet marker on the 3D map HUD.

5. Other Nonfunctional Requirements

5.1 Performance Requirements

Requirement IDRequirement Statement
NFR-001The system shall authenticate user credentials and issue a JWT within 1.5 seconds.
NFR-002The 3D Tactical Map HUD shall load and render extruded buildings within 3.0 seconds on launch.
NFR-003The geodesic distance calculation to nearby spawns shall execute in < 50 milliseconds on mobile hardware.
NFR-004The server-side ray-casting Point-in-Polygon validation shall execute in < 10 milliseconds per request.
NFR-005The backend server shall support at least 500 concurrent active student connections with latency < 200ms.
NFR-006The AR camera viewfinder shall maintain a stable rendering frame rate of at least 30 frames per second.

5.2 Safety Requirements

Requirement IDRequirement Statement
NFR-007The system shall display a mandatory pedestrian safety warning banner on launch reminding cadets to remain alert.
NFR-008The system shall strictly prohibit the placement of anomaly spawns on active roadways or construction zones.
NFR-009The system shall disengage camera AR mini-games if rapid vehicular transit speed (> 25 km/h) is detected.
NFR-010The system shall enforce a 15-meter interaction radius so cadets can interact without entering restricted zones.
NFR-011The mobile client shall throttle GPS sampling when battery charge falls below 15% to avoid sudden shutdown.

5.3 Security Requirements

Requirement IDRequirement Statement
NFR-012Passwords shall be hashed using BCrypt with a work factor ≥ 12 prior to database persistence.
NFR-013All client-server exchanges shall be encrypted using TLS 1.3 to prevent eavesdropping.
NFR-014API endpoints shall require a valid JWT Bearer token signed with HMAC-SHA256 and an environment secret.
NFR-015Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) shall enforce complete separation between Student Cadets and Administrators.
NFR-016All incoming text payloads shall be sanitized against regular expressions to prevent injection exploits.
NFR-017Spatial coordinates shall undergo dual validation: Pydantic schema bounds followed by ray-casting verification.

5.4 Software Quality Attributes

Requirement IDRequirement Statement
NFR-018The system shall provide a uniform responsive graphical interface across varied mobile screen resolutions.
NFR-019The database layer shall preserve ACID transactional guarantees across capture recordings and credit transfers.
NFR-020The frontend architecture shall follow clean component segregation supporting effortless addition of new mini-games.
NFR-021The backend shall return structured, machine-readable JSON error responses for all HTTP 4xx and 5xx conditions.
NFR-022The codebase shall maintain zero TypeScript compilation errors and pass all automated security test suites.

5.5 Business Rules

Rule IDBusiness Rule Statement
BR-001Only students and faculty with valid institutional registrations may participate in campus anomaly hunts.
BR-002An anomaly capture is valid if and only if the cadet's GPS coordinates fall within the CIT polygon and within 15m of the spawn.
BR-003Each anomaly spawn may only be captured once per student per spawn cycle to prevent XP farming.
BR-004A student's Energy gauge decreases by 10 units per capture attempt and automatically regenerates at 1 unit per minute.
BR-005Campus loot crates refresh at 06:00 and 18:00 daily across designated open campus sectors.
BR-006Student Cadets cannot access administrative telemetry or invoke spawn manipulation endpoints under any circumstance.
BR-007Active roaming buddy companions grant a passive +10% bonus to experience points earned within their home sector.
BR-008In-game Data Credits cannot be purchased with real fiat currency and are strictly earned through campus exploration.
BR-009Tag-Team Raids require a minimum squad size of 2 cadets located within 30 meters of the raid beacon.
BR-010Leaderboards compute cumulative student XP and department rankings, updating every 15 minutes.

6. Other Requirements

6.1 Database Requirements

The system utilizes a relational database structure with foreign-key referential integrity across users, captures, spawns, and friendships.

Requirement IDRequirement Statement
DB-001The database shall enforce unique constraints on email and username columns in the users table.
DB-002Foreign key constraints shall link captures.user_id to users.id with cascading integrity.
DB-003Primary keys across all tables shall utilize UUIDv4 36-character string identifiers.
DB-004The database shall store GPS coordinates using double-precision floating-point format (Float).
DB-005Timestamps across all tables shall be stored in UTC format using ISO 8601 representation.
DB-006Database queries for nearby spawns shall utilize indexing on coordinate and sector columns.

6.2 Backup and Recovery Requirements

The system shall execute daily automated snapshots of the database file, with point-in-time recovery capabilities and automatic reconnection upon backend service restarts.

6.3 Legal and Privacy Requirements

Location data is evaluated ephemerally in memory to verify geofence containment and distance, and is never logged as continuous tracking paths. Student data is protected in full compliance with institutional privacy policies.

6.4 Future Enhancements

Future releases will incorporate Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) direct peer battles, depth-mesh occlusion behind physical trees/pillars, and dynamic AI quest generation.
