# CampusQuest Privacy Note (For CIT Students)

Welcome to CampusQuest! Since this is an augmented reality game played on the physical CIT campus, your privacy and safety are our top priorities. Here is exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and who can see it.

## 1. What We Collect and Why

- **Location Data (GPS):** 
  - *Why:* To place anomalies around you and verify you are actually on campus when capturing them.
  - *Storage:* We **do not** track or store your continuous location history. Your coordinates are only saved momentarily when you successfully capture an anomaly (to record where it was caught) or when you deploy a multiplayer Tag-Team beacon (to show nearby cadets). 
  - *Retention:* Once a multiplayer raid ends, your real-time location is deleted from memory. 

- **Camera & AR Sensor Data:**
  - *Why:* To project the 3D holographic anomalies onto the real world using your device's camera.
  - *Storage:* We **do not** record video, take photos, or send camera feeds to our servers. The Augmented Reality processing happens entirely locally on your phone.

- **Account Profile:**
  - *Why:* To track your XP, Energy, Bestiary progression, and Department affiliation (e.g., CSE, ECE).
  - *Storage:* Your username, college email, and department are stored in our secure campus database.

## 2. Who Can See Your Data

- **Other Students:** Other players can see your `Username`, `Level`, and `Department` if you join a multiplayer Tag-Team Raid, or if you capture a Turf stronghold for your department. They will also see your rough location if you broadcast a co-op beacon.
- **Admin/Staff:** System administrators can view high-level metrics (total captures, total active cadets) to ensure the game is running smoothly. 

## 3. Data Deletion

You own your data. If you wish to delete your account, Bestiary records, and all associated capture history, please contact the CIT CampusQuest admin team. Your data will be permanently wiped from the database.

## 4. Safety Reminders

- **Stay Aware:** Do not play while driving, biking, or crossing roads.
- **Respect Boundaries:** Do not enter restricted labs, hostels, or staff areas just to catch an anomaly. Spawns are geofenced to public zones.

*(Note: This is a pilot phase document. Final legal and college-specific privacy policies may apply upon official launch.)*
